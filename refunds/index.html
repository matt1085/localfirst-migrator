Refund Policy

Operational refund policy for LocalFirst Migrator purchases.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Window

You may request a refund within 14 days of purchase if:

  • the product fails to install on the supported platform (Bazzite KDE, x86_64), or
  • a documented Flatpak migration or manual Flatpak sync cannot complete due to a product defect

What is not, by itself, a refund reason

  • Wanting KDE settings migration, Homebrew migration, Distrobox migration, or automatic background sync — these are disclosed as unsupported in v1
  • Wanting secret synchronization — secrets are never migrated
  • Using the product on an unsupported platform
  • Buyer’s remorse after a successful supported migration

How to request a refund

  1. Contact support through the support page or the payment provider’s receipt/order flow once checkout is live.
  2. Include your order reference, product version, and a short description of the install or Flatpak migration/sync failure.
  3. Where possible, attach a sanitized support report from Diagnostics (no secrets).

After a refund

Access to future download updates tied to that purchase may end. Local files already installed on your computers remain under your control; uninstall using the product uninstaller if desired.

Payment-provider rules and applicable consumer law may provide additional rights. This page describes the seller’s operational policy for LocalFirst Migrator.
